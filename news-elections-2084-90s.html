ELECTIONS 2084: THREE VISIONS

Thunder, Serenity and Freedom face voters as the Central Consensus Bureau begins final ballot processing.

Wire: SLOP Central Newsfeed • Date: Nov 4, 2084 • By: Staff Reporter #0429
3 MIN READ
Official State Portrait: Candidates Donald Thunder, Luna Serenity and Bobby Freedom awaiting final ballot processing.

The 2084 presidential election opened Tuesday morning following a final system check by the Central Consensus Bureau.

The three candidates on the ballot are Donald Thunder, Luna Serenity and Bobby Freedom, representing three different approaches to national policy, automation and citizen management.

Thunder has campaigned heavily on border security and automated defense, while Serenity has focused on technological integration and her Personal Progress Score program. Freedom has based his campaign around national discipline and a return to what his campaign describes as “human-scale government.”

BORDER SECURITY & AUTOMATED DEFENSE

DONALD THUNDER

Thunder has campaigned heavily on border security and automated defense systems. His platform prioritizes fortified perimeter boundaries, automated surveillance grids, and high-readiness national deterrence.

“A secure border and automated defense are the foundation of national security.”
PLATFORM: BORDER SECURITY & AUTOMATED DEFENSE
TECHNOLOGICAL INTEGRATION & PROGRESS

LUNA SERENITY

Serenity has focused her campaign on technological integration and the widespread adoption of her Personal Progress Score program, advocating for automated public services and synchronized citizen welfare.

“Technological integration enables a coordinated, supportive society for every citizen.”
PLATFORM: PERSONAL PROGRESS SCORE & INTEGRATION
NATIONAL DISCIPLINE & TRADITION

BOBBY FREEDOM

Freedom has based his campaign around national discipline, traditional civic structures, and a decisive return to what his campaign describes as “human-scale government.”

“Order, national discipline, and a return to human-scale governance.”
PLATFORM: HUMAN-SCALE GOVERNMENT & DISCIPLINE

Voting is being conducted through the Unified Citizen Network. Officials said the system has been tested across all registered sectors and is currently operating without major interruptions.

The Bureau did not comment on reports from several districts claiming that ballots had been registered before voters arrived at polling terminals.

“A normal synchronization feature.”

— Central Consensus Bureau Spokesperson

A spokesperson later described the reports as “a normal synchronization feature.”

Final results are expected after the Consensus Bureau completes its verification process.

The three candidates have not yet commented on the current tally.
