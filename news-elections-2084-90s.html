ELECTIONS 2084

THREE VISIONS. ONE STRONGER TOMORROW.™

Wire: SLOP Central Newsfeed • Date: Nov 4, 2084 • By: Staff Reporter #0429
3 MIN READ
Official State Portrait: The three candidates awaiting transmission from the Central Consensus Bureau.

The 2084 election is finally here.

Three candidates.
Three visions.
One future.

At least, that's what they say.

Millions of citizens across all twelve sectors are now being asked to choose who gets to run the country, the economy, the military, the media, and basically whatever is left of real life.

Network telemetry confirms voter participation hubs have opened across every residential quadrant. Below are the three registered contenders:

MAKE TOMORROW LOUDER.™

DONALD THUNDER™

Donald Thunder says the future needs more strength, more borders, and more power. His campaign says strong countries make strong people. His plan includes bigger military systems, bigger national borders, and bigger everything.

When asked what should happen to citizens who disagree with him, Thunder simply replied: “They can disagree louder.”
CAMPAIGN MESSAGE: STRENGTH BRINGS PEACE.™
PEOPLE. PROGRESS. FOREVER.™

LUNA SERENITY™

Luna Serenity wants a softer future. More technology. More equality. More progress. Her campaign promises a kinder society where humans and machines work together. She also supports universal digital identity, automated government services, and a new system where every citizen gets a Personal Progress Score™.

The score will help the government “understand what you need.” Nobody really knows what that means. But it sounds good.
CAMPAIGN MESSAGE: A KINDER. STRONGER. HAPPIER HUMANITY.™
TRADITION LIVES LONGER.™

BOBBY FREEDOM™

Bobby Freedom says the future has gone too far. He wants order. He wants tradition. He wants people to remember “the old way.” His campaign promises stronger national discipline, stricter social rules, and a return to traditional values.

His supporters say society needs one nation, one order, no distractions. His critics say the plan sounds a lot like a dictatorship. Bobby says that's fake news.
CAMPAIGN MESSAGE: DISCIPLINE PRESERVES FREEDOM.™
