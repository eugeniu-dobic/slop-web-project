Government Moves City 12 After Residents Complain About the View

The City of New Miami has officially been relocated 73 miles south to optimize sunset engagement.

Wire: SLOP Infrastructure & Continental Transit Desk • Date: Nov 7, 2084
2 MIN READ
Department of Municipal Mobility Telemetry: City 12 crawler tracks traversing the southern coastline.

The city of New Miami has officially been moved 73 miles south.

Following a wave of telemetry reports and negative citizen reviews, officials determined the original municipal coordinates suffered from fatal design flaws:

  • Bad weather
  • Low tourism
  • Ugly sunsets
  • Poor engagement

The government confirmed that physically moving the entire city was significantly cheaper than fixing it.

Approximately 4.8 million people were relocated overnight.

Most citizens didn't notice.

Their homes, roads and schools were moved digitally first through centralized cloud spatial remapping. Physical relocation followed three weeks later when twelve massive hydraulic crawler tracks hoisted the urban platform and walked it down the coastline.

The city's new location is now closer to the ocean.

Very close.

Environmental experts warn that New Miami is now partially underwater at daily high tide.

The government disagrees.

“It's not underwater. It's beachfront.”

— Official Statement, Department of Municipal Mobility & Coastal Elevation

Real estate prices are already up 340% as brokers promote submerged ground-floor apartments as exclusive sub-aquatic luxury suites.

Breaking update: New Miami has been moved again. This time because regional investors liked the old location better.

Citizens are advised: Please remain calm. Your city may move.
