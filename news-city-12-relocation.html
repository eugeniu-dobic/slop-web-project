CITY 12 MOVED AFTER VIEW COMPLAINTS

Relocation follows months of complaints regarding skyline visibility and unauthorized horizon obstruction.

Wire: SLOP Infrastructure & Continental Transit Desk • Date: Nov 7, 2084 • By: Staff Reporter #0318
2 MIN READ
Municipal Planning Authority Telemetry: Automated infrastructure units repositioning the central districts of City 12 during overnight grid disconnect.

City 12 has been relocated approximately 4.7 kilometers east following a protracted series of formal complaints regarding restricted views of the western skyline.

The massive geographic shift was formally approved by the Municipal Planning Authority last month and completed in a single overnight operation. Planning officials stated that the previous coordinates of the municipality created “unnecessary visual interference” and unauthorized horizon obstruction for residents situated across several designated premium housing sectors.

Despite the metropolitan scale of the maneuver, municipal authorities confirmed that zero buildings were demolished during the operation. Instead, the municipality was temporarily disconnected from the national transport grid and regional utility relays while automated infrastructure units and pneumatic stabilizers repositioned the central districts along designated civic rails.

Civic alerts instructed residents across all affected sectors to remain indoors between 02:00 and 05:00 while the final geographic locking sequence was executed by heavy transit drones.

“This was a minor geographic adjustment to restore optimal line-of-sight compliance. The vast majority of citizens will not notice a difference.”

— Official Spokesperson, Municipal Planning Authority

By sunrise, transport connections were restored, though several residents reported waking up to discover that their registered home coordinates and localized map readings had fundamentally changed overnight.

When questioned regarding the unexpected cartographic shift, the Authority confirmed that the coordinate alteration was entirely expected and normal for an automated repositioning maneuver.

Residents who disagree with the relocation or wish to contest their revised morning sun angle can submit a formal complaint through the Civic Satisfaction Portal.

According to the portal's automated ticketing ledger, the current processing queue for relocation grievances is estimated at approximately 19 months.
