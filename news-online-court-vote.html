Too many lawyers. Too many judges. Too much boring paperwork.

The entire trial process has been reduced to one fundamental question:

Did this person do it?

The system has already become the nation's highest-grossing media franchise. Last night's headline trial peaked at over 2.7 billion simultaneous viewers across planetary networks.

The defendant was found guilty with 94% of the popular vote and sentenced on the spot.

Subsequent forensic review hours later confirmed that the individual was entirely innocent.

Officials insist the system functioned exactly as intended.
