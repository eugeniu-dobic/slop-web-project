COURTS DECIDE GUILT BY ONLINE VOTE™

New public verdict system expands following successful pilot program in 14 districts.

Wire: SLOP National Court Network & Civic Justice Desk • Date: Nov 8, 2084 • By: Staff Reporter #0512
2 MIN READ
National Court Network Telemetry: Public verdict terminal processing digital juror ballots during the 14-district pilot program.

The National Court Network has officially expanded its public verdict system nationwide following an extensive six-month pilot program conducted across 14 metropolitan districts.

Under the newly authorized procedure, registered citizens are empowered to review selected municipal cases and submit a digital verdict before the court reaches a final decision.

Rather than relying on a traditional raw tally, the system implements an algorithmic weighted voting model based on citizen reliability scores and previous participation metrics. Ballots cast by individuals with superior civic standing carry calibrated weighting in determining the final verdict outcome.

Officials said the system is intended to increase public involvement while dramatically reducing court processing times across the nation's congested judicial infrastructure.

During the pilot program, average verdict times fell from 11 days of formal proceedings to just under three hours.

“These accounts were not bots. They were classified as temporary citizens.”

— Official Clarification, Justice Ministry

The expansion has nevertheless raised concerns among critics regarding the quality of public participation. Observers pointed to several high-profile cases during the pilot that received large numbers of decisive votes originating from accounts registered less than 24 hours earlier.

The Justice Ministry dismissed allegations of automated manipulation, confirming that the newly registered accounts were not bots and had been formally designated as “temporary citizens” authorized to participate under emergency judicial throughput protocols.

The first nationwide vote will begin this Friday across all participating federal and municipal court circuits.

Citizens with an active Civic Participation subscription will receive automatic access to the digital verdict portal, alongside instant docket updates and streamlined ballot processing.
