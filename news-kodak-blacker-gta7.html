KODAK BLACKER™ CONFIRMED FOR GTA 7

New synthetic performer reportedly approved after studio completes “likeness and attitude testing.”

Wire: SLOP Gaming & Culture Desk • Date: Nov 5, 2084 • By: Staff Entertainment Reporter
2 MIN READ
Promotional asset: Synthetic performer Kodak Blacker™ approved for release in GTA 7 following studio likeness and attitude evaluation protocols.

Rockstar Media Group has confirmed that Kodak Blacker™, a fully synthetic performer, will appear in the upcoming release of GTA 7.

The character was reportedly developed using a combination of licensed performance data, archived interviews and machine-generated personality models.

According to the studio, Blacker™ will perform several original tracks and appear throughout the game's Miami-inspired districts.

The announcement has already generated discussion among players, particularly over the decision to credit the character as a separate artist rather than as an AI production.

“Not an imitation of any individual — a new entertainment identity optimized for the 2084 audience.”

— Rockstar Media Group Representative

A company representative said the performer is “not an imitation of any individual” and was instead created as “a new entertainment identity optimized for the 2084 audience.”

The character's contract reportedly includes automatic royalty payments to the platform whenever a player listens to one of his songs.

Rockstar has not announced whether Blacker™ will be available as a playable character.

Pre-orders for the game opened this morning.

The trailer currently has 84 million views. 61 million of them were generated by verified automated accounts.
